Mass price update for product pricelists

This module adds a "Mass price update" tool to product pricelists. From a pricelist's form view, open a wizard that increases or decreases, by a percentage or a fixed amount, the price of every Product-level rule in that pricelist. Optionally, the starting price for each product can be copied from another ("reference") pricelist before the adjustment is applied.

Before writing any price, the wizard shows a preview of the resulting prices so changes can be reviewed and confirmed or discarded.
